1. **Hand-written `0001` migration instead of `alembic revision --autogenerate`.** The autogenerate variant was tested but the hand-written revision was preferred for two reasons: (a) it pins an explicit, reviewable baseline rather than relying on live-DB diffing against the Neon pooled endpoint (which can be flaky); (b) it guarantees column-for-column parity with `app/models/user.py`. To avoid a destructive `CREATE TABLE` attempt, both were issued `alembic stamp 0001` — a one-time, non-destructive baseline marker. Fresh environments will apply `0001` normally.

| Blueprint method | Implementation | Notes |
